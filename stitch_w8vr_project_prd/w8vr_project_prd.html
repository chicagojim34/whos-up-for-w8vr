PRD: Who’s Up for W8VR? (Project Codename) Author: [Your Name/Role] Status: Draft / For Review Date: March 24, 2026 1. Executive Summary Social isolation and the "activation energy" required to organize group hangouts are at an all-time high. Existing tools are either too professional (LinkedIn/Eventbrite), too broad (Facebook), or too focused on 1:1 interaction (iMessage/WhatsApp). The Core Problem: Group messages (WhatsApp/iMessage) create "Coordination Chaos." Hosts struggle to track RSVPs across dozens of texts, while participants are bombarded with notifications for events they aren't attending. W8VR (pronounced "Whatever") is a mobile-first platform that eliminates this noise, providing a streamlined intent-broadcast system for friend circles and public communities. 2. Goals & Objectives Primary Goal: Increase the frequency of real-world social interactions by reducing coordination friction. Success Metrics: - L7/L28 Engagement: Number of events joined per user per month. - Noise Reduction: % decrease in non-essential notifications compared to baseline group chats. - Host Conversion: % of users who move from "Joiner" to "Host." - Friction Metric: Time from "Event Created" to "Capacity Reached." 3. User Personas - The Catalyst (Host): Wants to organize but dreads the "who's in?" follow-up chase. - The Joiner (User): Wants to see what's happening but is "muted" on most group chats. - The Explorer (Public): Looking for community-led events without needing to be in the "inner circle." 4. Functional Requirements 4.1. Core User Features (The Joiner) - Unified Feed (P0): Ranked list of events from "Joined Circles" and "Public". - Event Categories (P0): Filter/Browse by type (Sports, Dining, etc.). - Smart Muting (P0): Non-RSVPs are excluded from updates. - Notification Tiers (P1): Only "Close Friends" new events or "Yes" RSVP updates. - Discovery Map (P1): Map-view of events. - My Schedule (P2): Calendar view. 4.2. Hosting & Logistics (The Catalyst) - Event Creator (P0): Simple What/Where/When flow. - Segmented Broadcast (P0): Updates to specific RSVP groups. - Response Dashboard (P0): Real-time list of Yes/No/Maybe/Waitlist. - Capacity Management (P0): Max spots + auto-waitlist. - Privacy Toggles (P0): Private, Hidden, or Open. - Waitlist Promotion (P1): Auto-notification for replacements. 4.3. Social Graph & Identity - Circle Management (P0): Create/Join "Circles". - Contact Sync (P0): Match mobile contacts. - Safety Tools (P0): Block, report, and remove. - Group Sharing (P1): Deep links/QR codes. 5. Event Taxonomy Categories: Active, Dining, Entertainment, Home/Social, Creative, Professional, Community. 6. Design Language 6.1. Visual Principles - Frictionless: Minimal text; use cards and chips. - Status-Aware: Cards change based on RSVP status. - Action-Oriented: "Post an Event" button is most prominent. 6.2. The Palette - Primary: Electric Indigo (#5D5FEF) - Secondary: Soft Mint (#D1FAE5) - Quiet: Dusty Gray (#9CA3AF) - Surface: Off-White (#F9FAFB) 6.3. Component Specs - The Event Card: Top: Image/Icon; Middle: Title + Proximity; Bottom: Status Badge. - The Status Ring: Circular progress bar showing capacity (Green=Open, Red=Full). 7. Technical Considerations - Communication Model: Announcement platform, not chat. "Logistic Threads" for comments. - Non-Goal: Full-featured IM. Deep-link to WhatsApp/iMessage for long-form social. - Data Privacy: Private addresses hidden until "Yes" RSVP.
